Optimize configurations
=======================

Compare candidate configurations, inspect measured trade-offs, and reuse the best observed result.

Our team also develops internal optimization agents that can find strong configurations with fewer workload evaluations
than grid search. These agents are separate from the public distribution.

Start with the public grid-search example below, or :doc:`integrate your own selection policy </extend/agent>`.
